About BettermanAI

Your Personal Workflow Optimizer

BettermanAI is an intelligent assistant that observes how you work, identifies patterns in your behavior, and helps you automate repetitive tasks.

How It Works
Learn

BettermanAI observes your mouse movements, keyboard activity, and window interactions to understand your workflow.

Detect

Pattern detection algorithms identify repetitive sequences in your behavior.

Suggest

When a pattern is detected multiple times, BettermanAI suggests potential automations.

Automate

With your approval, automations are created to handle repetitive tasks for you.

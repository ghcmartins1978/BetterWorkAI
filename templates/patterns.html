Understanding Pattern Detection

BettermanAI uses advanced pattern recognition algorithms to identify repetitive sequences in your behavior. Patterns are evaluated based on frequency, consistency, and automation potential.

Confidence Score

Higher scores indicate more consistent patterns that have been observed multiple times. Patterns with scores above 70% are good candidates for automation.

Pattern Status

Patterns start as "Detected", then move to "Evaluating" as they're analyzed. They become "Approved" if they're suitable for automation, or "Rejected" if not.

Suggestions

Approved patterns generate automation suggestions. You can review and accept them to create macros automatically.

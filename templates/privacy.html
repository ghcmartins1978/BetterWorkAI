Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

BettermanAI is built with privacy as a core principle. All your data stays on your computer, and you have full control over monitoring settings.

What Information We Collect

To provide automation capabilities, BettermanAI collects the following categories of information:

  • Mouse & Keyboard Interactions: Mouse movements, clicks, and keyboard input (not content)
  • Window Information: Active window titles and transitions between windows
  • Application Settings: Your preferences and settings for the application
How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Pattern Detection: To identify repetitive workflows and tasks in your behavior
  • Automation Suggestion: To suggest and create automations that can save you time
  • Macro Recording & Playback: To record your actions and replay them later
  • Performance Analytics: Internal usage statistics to improve the application
Data Storage & Security

All collected data is stored only on your local device. BettermanAI does not transmit your activity data to any external server.

Local Storage

All user data, including recorded events, patterns, and automations, are stored in a local database on your computer.

Data Retention

You can control how long your data is kept in the Settings page. By default, event data is kept for 30 days.

AI Usage

BettermanAI uses OpenAI's GPT API for intelligent automation suggestions. When this feature is enabled, only abstract pattern data is sent to the API, not your actual activity data.

You can disable AI-powered suggestions in Settings and use only the local heuristic methods for pattern detection and automation suggestions.

Your Privacy Controls

BettermanAI gives you full control over your privacy. You can:

  • Enable or disable monitoring at any time
  • Control which types of events are recorded (mouse, keyboard, windows)
  • Set automatic data deletion rules
  • Disable AI-powered features
  • Clear all stored data
